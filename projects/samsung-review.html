Background

Too many models, too little time

Apple releases 4 flagship models a year; Samsung releases 5. With smartphone users typically upgrading every 2–4 years, buyers are left wading through reviews across e-commerce listings and YouTube before every purchase — a paradox of choice that stretches out lead conversion time.

Dataset

800+ reviews per rating tier, per model

The dataset was built through web scraping on Amazon US, collecting over 800 reviews for each rating tier across multiple Samsung phone models — a broad enough base to train and validate a topic model with confidence.

Methodology

From raw text to clustered topics

Review text was cleaned and preprocessed, then passed through FASTopic for topic clustering — grouping thousands of individual reviews per phone into a handful of clear, recurring strengths and weaknesses instead of leaving a buyer to read them one by one.

Phone strengths — word cloud

  • “good”, “great”, “excellent” and “love” show up as the positive sentiment words
  • “battery” and “camera” stand out as the features most tied to positive reviews
  • General words such as “phone” and “good” are removed after preprocessing, showing that stopwords were used to focus the data on phone specifications such as “camera” and “battery”
  • Lemmatization is also visible, for example “scratches” → “scratch”

Phone weaknesses — word cloud

  • “issue” and “problem” show up as the negative sentiment words
  • “phone” and “screen” are the most frequent words
  • “locked” and “unlock” can indicate device security problems
  • “Amazon”, “seller”, “return” and “refund” can indicate customer dissatisfaction with the transaction experience
  • After preprocessing, the word cloud for negative reviews becomes more focused and clearly highlights users’ complaints
  • Words such as “issue”, “problem”, “return”, “fix”, “charge” and “repair” become more apparent, pointing to complaints about device malfunctions, battery/charging issues and after-sales service

Conclusions

A pipeline that's ready to plug in

  • The project delivers an end-to-end NLP pipeline that turns raw reviews into clear strength/weakness summaries per phone model using FASTopic
  • The average coherence score confirms the model is solid enough for industry deployment, not just a proof of concept

Business Recommendations

Where this plugs into the business

E-commerce AI assistance

Embed the model in an Amazon-style AI assistant to summarize customer reviews automatically, improving accuracy and reliability over manual reading.

Lower market research costs

Brands can access clustered reviews directly instead of commissioning separate market surveys to gauge product strengths and weaknesses.

Real-time complaint monitoring

Surface customer complaints as they emerge so brand reputation and satisfaction issues get handled before they compound.

Higher lead conversion

Giving buyers a condensed, trustworthy summary up front shortens the research phase and speeds up the path to purchase.
