Background

Fast fashion moves fast — or you lose

Fast-fashion players like ZARA, H&M, SHEIN, UNIQLO, and Lululemon live and die by how quickly they read and respond to shifting trends. Misreading a season can mean significant losses or missed revenue, and the competition is intense: SHEIN alone pushes out 10,000 new designs a day.

Dataset

ZARA retail sales records

Transaction-level sales data across ZARA's retail categories, store sections, and seasonal stock cycles, used to trace which product lines and store layouts actually drive revenue.

Methodology

A full Dockerized ETL pipeline

Raw data was loaded into PostgreSQL through Docker, then retrieved and preprocessed via an Apache Airflow DAG workflow. The cleaned output was pushed to ElasticSearch as a NoSQL store, with the final visualization layer built in Kibana — a setup that mirrors how a real retail analytics team would operationalize this kind of reporting.

Results

What's actually selling

  • Jackets remain ZARA's best-performing category, accounting for 67% of total revenue
  • Shoe revenue has overtaken T-shirt revenue, signaling strong and growing demand
  • ZARA customers show low price sensitivity, strong buying power, and high brand loyalty
  • The Aisle section drives the highest unit volume of any area in the store
  • Sweaters carry the highest seasonal deadstock ratio of any category

Conclusions

A loyal customer base with room to expand

The data points to a customer base that's already loyal and willing to spend — the opportunity is less about winning new customers and more about expanding what loyal customers already buy, while fixing layout and inventory inefficiencies.

Business Recommendations

Where to focus next

Boost shoes and women's fashion

Expanding jacket, sweater, and shoe lines for women could meaningfully lift total revenue by year end.

Rotate product placement

Moving slower-selling items to the Aisle or End-Cap areas — the store's highest-traffic zones — could lift their sales volume.

Reduce low-selling stock

Right-size T-shirt and jeans inventory to avoid deadstock, or move it via bundling (e.g. an extra 15% off on a $200+ purchase).

Strengthen loyalty membership

Retaining already-loyal customers protects market share and increases spend per person over time.
