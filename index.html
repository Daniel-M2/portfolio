Section 01 — About

From the production line to the data pipeline

A Food Technology degree taught me how a system behaves under real-world variance. Data science gave me a better set of instruments to measure it.

I started out managing daily production for a chocolate manufacturer — leading a floor team, tracking yield, and chasing down the causes of defects until the numbers held. That work made me obsessive about clean data: if the input isn't trustworthy, no dashboard downstream will save you.

That obsession is why I pivoted into data science. I'm now building the analytical and machine learning skillset — Python, SQL, statistical testing, model deployment — to do at scale what I used to do manually on a factory floor: find the variable that's actually driving the outcome, and turn it into a decision someone can act on.
